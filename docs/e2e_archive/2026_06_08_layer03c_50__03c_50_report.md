# Layer 03c (Acoustic Prosody) — 50-Clip Smell Test

**Date**: June 8, 2026
**Goal**: Validate the 03c Acoustic Prosody pipeline (emotion2vec+ SER + SenseVoice AED + librosa) end-to-end on 50 filtered clips, following the 03a/03b smell-test pattern: show the highest-scoring, lowest-scoring, and a failed-to-score clip, and spot-check that the results make sense.
**Host**: Mac Studio (M4 Max, 64 GB), MPS.

## Sample
The **same 50-clip manifest used for the 03b smell test** (`e2e_reports/2026_06_04_layer03b_50/manifest_03b_50.json`, copied here as `manifest_03c_50.json`). Because 03c consumes the same `task_reaction_window_sec` as 03b and that manifest already had climax metadata populated, **03c added ~zero climax cost** — it only slices audio in the bounded reaction window. Models: `iic/emotion2vec_plus_large` (SER) + `iic/SenseVoiceSmall` (AED), both from the offline ModelScope cache; FunASR 1.3.1.

## 1. The model works — real, varied acoustic signal
On the 21 audio-present clips, emotion2vec+ produces **genuine, confident, varied** distributions (happy / sad / disgusted / other / neutral), not noise — confidence ranges up to **0.99**. The pipeline is functionally correct end-to-end: ffmpeg slices the reaction window → librosa features (dBFS, pitch variance) → emotion2vec+ 9-class → heuristic tone/scalar, with SenseVoice gated on low-confidence samples. No fail-fast, no crashes, resumable atomic writes.

## 2. Low yield — a data-modality limit (29/50 clips have no audio)
**Only 21/50 clips carry an audio track**; the other 29 (ffmpeg exits non-zero on `-vn` extraction → caught) fall back to a Neutral stub. This is 03c's analog of 03b's distant-face problem: a large fraction of egocentric Ego4D `full_scale` clips simply have no usable audio, so the layer is intrinsically low-yield on this corpus. Of the 21 that do have audio, **11 produced a non-Neutral tone** (8 Soothing, 2 Alarming, 1 Discouraging).

## 3. Exemplars + spot-check
Spot-check audio slices were extracted to `audio/` and re-scored with emotion2vec+ to confirm the pipeline's numbers reproduce exactly (the user can listen to the `.wav`s against the scores).

### Highest-scoring — `6b3988dd` "Grocery shopping indoors" → Soothing (+1.0)
`happy = 0.99`, dBFS −9.8. The most confident result in the set; a clearly cheerful vocal slice. Spot-check reproduces `happy=0.99` exactly. ✅ makes sense.

### Lowest-scoring — `573fc64b` "Cooking" → Alarming (−1.0)
Classified Alarming, **but the spot-check exposes a problem**: the actual top emotion is `other=0.82` with `angry`/`fearful` ≈ 0 (dBFS −11.9). It is flagged Alarming **only because the loud-volume bonus (+0.3) fires** on noisy cooking sounds — not because of any alarmed *voice*. See Issue 2. (The other Alarming clip, `04f2cec1`, is the same story: `disgusted 0.41`, loud.) ⚠️ tone is right-signed by luck, wrong cause.

### Failed-to-score — `630bd4ba` "Hiking" → Neutral stub
No audio track; ffmpeg extraction fails (rc=234) → the task is emitted as `max_amplitude_dbFS = −100.0, dominant_emotion = "neutral", confidence = 1.0, tone = "Neutral"`. **Indistinguishable from a genuinely-quiet clip** — see Issue 1.

### Bonus — cross-layer corroboration on `43bd06f3` "Cleaning / laundry"
03c reads `sad = 0.90 → Discouraging (−0.5)`. **This is the same clip 03b flagged with sadness** (03b agg −0.09). Two independent modalities (face emotion + vocal prosody) agreeing on the same negative affect is exactly the multi-layer corroboration the architecture is designed for. ✅ strong validation.

## 4. Issues surfaced (filed in docs/03c)
1. **No-audio clips report as *confident* Neutral.** A clip with no audio track emits `confidence = 1.0`, `tone = "Neutral"` — identical to a genuinely-neutral clip — so downstream consumers cannot tell "no data" from "confident neutral." 29/50 clips here. (Issue 1, recommended fix: emit a distinct `no_audio` marker / `skipped_reason`, mirroring 03a's `NoFace` and the 03b honesty gate.)
2. **Loud ambient task noise → false Alarming.** The `high_volume_bonus` (+0.3) can make Alarming the winning tone even when `angry+fearful ≈ 0` (both Alarming clips here). In egocentric footage the loudest sound is usually the wearer's own manipulation noise. (Issue 2: gate the volume bonus on a minimum `angry+fearful`, or on speech presence.)
3. **Ambient audio ≠ bystander reaction.** 03c scores all ambient sound in the window, which egocentrically is dominated by the camera-wearer, so the emotion may not be the bystander's. (Issue 3, conceptual — analog of 03e's gaze-vs-head-pose.)

## Verdict
- **03c is functionally correct** and emits real, confident, varied acoustic emotion on clips that have audio; it cross-corroborates 03b on a shared clip.
- **It is intrinsically low-yield on this corpus** (21/50 have audio) — a data-modality limit, now documented.
- **Two honesty/precision gaps** worth fixing before a full-corpus run: the confident-Neutral no-audio stub (Issue 1) and the volume-bonus false-Alarming (Issue 2).
