## 1. Executive Summary

## Designer note (November 4, 2026; post hoc, not part of the pre-registered verdict)

The verdict above follows the pre-registered rule, and the implementation was verified to match the spec. Details are in `05_wave_b_reflex.md` §7; the next step is Issue 12 in `ongoing_general_errors.md`.
- **The PROCEED clause is a window-length artifact.**
  - `fail` reaction windows average 45.7 frames vs. 35.5 for `pre`, and `reflex-face-v1`'s `max` and `std` features grow with the number of frames.
  - Window length alone scores **0.735 [0.675, 0.796]**. The face features' means alone score 0.515 [0.471, 0.564].
  - With equal-length windows, `react-face` scores **0.525 [0.473, 0.572]** (start-anchored) and **0.511 [0.457, 0.561]** (end-anchored).
- **The "action-only" video shows the participant's silhouette** (posture and gestures), so the action-only controls also see body reactions.
- **§3.3's step-prior explanation is wrong.**
  - The counts it quotes (`475 / 478`, `13 / 126`) are not in the data, and there is no `Explain` column; the column is `Explanation Level`.
  - Actual resolution rates by explanation level are high 165/187, medium 115/143, low 106/143, zero 102/131.
  - The outcome follows the **step** instead: pick 219/219, carry 198/220, place 71/165. Item B4b regenerates that paragraph from the data.
- **`react-full` "not run: annotations carry no transcripts" is a spec error.** `speech.csv` holds the transcripts in a column named `Text`, while the spec named `Transcript`.
- **The `react-nonverbal:shuffled` FAIL is a single-seed false alarm.** The 50-seed null was reproduced by the designer (mean 0.5100, SD 0.0626; seed 0 = 0.6025).
